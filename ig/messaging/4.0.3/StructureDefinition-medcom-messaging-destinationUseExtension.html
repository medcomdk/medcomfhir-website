Extension: MedComDestinationUseExtension

Official URL: http://medcomfhir.dk/ig/messaging/StructureDefinition/medcom-messaging-destinationUseExtension Version: 4.0.3
Active as of 2026-11-11 Computable Name: MedComMessagingDestinationUseExtension
