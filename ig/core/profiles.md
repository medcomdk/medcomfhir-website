# Profiles - DK MedCom Core v1.0.0-test

## Profiles

The following profiles are defined in DK MedCom Core.

* [MedComCoreCareTeam](StructureDefinition-medcom-core-careteam.md) 
Careteam participating in the care of a patient 

* [MedComCoreDiagnosticReport](StructureDefinition-medcom-core-diagnosticreport.md) 
Report containing all observation results. 

* [MedComCoreEncounter](StructureDefinition-medcom-core-encounter.md) 
An interaction between a patient/citizen and healthcare provider(s) for the purpose of providing healthcare service(s) or assessing the health status of a patient/citizen. 

* [MedComCoreObservation](StructureDefinition-medcom-core-observation.md) 
Observation profile. 

* [MedComCoreOrganization](StructureDefinition-medcom-core-organization.md) 
A formally recognized organizations within danish healthcare, registered with a SOR identifier issued by The Danish Health Data Authority 

* [MedComCorePatient](StructureDefinition-medcom-core-patient.md) 
Patient/citizen resource to use as subject in MedCom communication. 

* [MedComCorePractitioner](StructureDefinition-medcom-core-practitioner.md) 
Practitioner resource is used to describe a healthcare professional or another actor involved in citizen or patient care. 

* [MedComCorePractitionerRole](StructureDefinition-medcom-core-practitionerrole.md) 
PractitionerRole resource used to describe the role of a healthcare professional or another actor involved in citizen or patient care. 

* [MedComMedia](StructureDefinition-medcom-core-media.md) 
Profile to capture relevant images,audio, video and document as part of the healthcare process
